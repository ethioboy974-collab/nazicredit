SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective September 1, 2026

Program description

NaziCredit sends transactional text messages to customers who have expressly agreed to receive an SMS about their pickup order. Messages may include confirmation that an order is ready for pickup or closely related order-status information. The program does not send marketing messages.

Consent

Before recording consent, a NaziCredit employee asks the customer for permission to send an order-ready text. Participation is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Customers who decline can still place and collect orders.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on order activity. Typically, a customer receives one ready-for-pickup message for an order. Message and data rates may apply according to the customer’s wireless plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out of future SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance or contact the store directly. After an opt-out request, the customer may receive a final confirmation message. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Eligibility and delivery

Customers must provide a valid mobile number they are authorized to use. Delivery depends on participating wireless carriers and network availability and is not guaranteed.

Privacy

Our handling of mobile information and consent records is described in the NaziCredit Privacy Policy. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Changes

We may update or discontinue the SMS program as permitted by law. The effective date above identifies the current version of these terms.
