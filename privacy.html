Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Information we collect

When a customer places an order, NaziCredit may collect the customer’s name, mobile phone number, order details, pickup time, and whether the customer agreed to receive an order-ready text message.

How we use information

We use this information to create and fulfill orders, contact customers about those orders, provide customer support, protect the service, and maintain business records. SMS messages are transactional order-status messages, not marketing messages.

Mobile information and SMS consent

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS consent records, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers only as needed to deliver messages and operate the service.

Message frequency varies based on the number of orders a customer places. Message and data rates may apply. Customers may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS messages is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

Data retention and security

We retain order and consent records only as reasonably needed for operations, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Choices

Customers may decline SMS consent and still place an order. A customer may opt out of future messages by replying STOP or by contacting the store directly.

Updates

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
